measuring

  1. Mark two anchor points A and B on the walls with tape. Measure A to B and type it in.
  2. For every new point: tap 2 already-fixed points on the plan, type the measured distance to each, press OK.
  3. Two positions match those distances - the marked one is placed. Tap the other candidate, or press flip, if it is on the wrong side.
  4. Extra measurements between existing points: select the 2 points, type the distance, press record. Residuals show per point; edit any measurement in the log.

walls, items, 3D

In walls mode tap points in order; tapping the first point again closes the room. In items mode create an item, then place it by two measured distances to a corner, on a wall (offset from a wall end), or drop it and drag in move mode. The 3D button shows everything extruded at real heights.

units

A number with a decimal point is metres, without is centimetres: 342 = 3.42 m, 3.42 = 3.42 m, 84 = 84 cm, 15. = 15 m.

keys

Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, double-tap empty plan to zoom in one-handed. Keyboard: digits, Enter = OK, Tab = other field, f = flip, Escape = cancel, ctrl+z undo.

Everything is saved on this device automatically. Export JSON from the log sheet for backup or to move devices.
