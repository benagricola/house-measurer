how it works

  1. Mark two anchor points A and B on the walls with tape. Measure A to B and type it in.
  2. For every new point: tap 2 already-fixed points on the plan, type the measured distance to each, press OK.
  3. Two positions match those distances - the marked one is placed. Tap the other candidate, or press flip, if it is on the wrong side.

units

A number with a decimal point is metres, without is centimetres: 342 = 3.42 m, 3.42 = 3.42 m, 84 = 84 cm. For a whole number of metres add a dot: 15. = 15 m.

keys

Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. On a keyboard: digits, Enter = OK, Tab = other field, f = flip, ctrl+z undo.

Everything is saved on this device automatically. Undo covers every step.
