measuring

  1. Mark two anchor points A and B (ideally corners) with tape. Measure A to B and type it in.
  2. For every new point: tap 2 already-fixed points, type the measured distance to each, press OK. Two positions match - the marked one is placed; tap the other candidate or press flip if it is on the wrong side.
  3. For a stronger fix, tap up to 4 references (spread apart as seen from where you stand - rays crossing at 60-120 degrees are ideal) and enter the distances one at a time. Extra distances average the laser noise down, expose a bad reading as a residual, and pick the correct side automatically.
  4. New points are reference-only by default (walling: off). Fix reference points around the space first - especially ones that can see what A and B cannot (through doorways, past corners) - then build each room in walls mode: tap the corners in order, tap the first again to close, and give the ceiling height.
  5. Prefer chaining instead? Toggle walling: on and each new point links into the wall outline as you measure, until the room closes (turning it on before any outline exists starts the run at A-B). Fix a wrong link in walls mode with step back; if a reference point slipped into the outline, select it and press unwall - the loop reroutes past it and the point stays usable.
  6. Placed a point on the wrong side and only noticed later? Tap it so it is the only selected point and press flip - it mirrors to the other candidate and everything measured from it re-solves. If those dependents landed mirrored as well, press flip a second time and they mirror too.
  7. The survey check (the amber/red pill in the header when something needs attention, or the button in the data sheet) diagnoses problems in plain words - unsolved points, disagreeing measurements, weak fix angles - and lists the exact steps to fix each, including which existing point to shoot from for the strongest improvement.
  8. Extra check measurements (e.g. a room diagonal): select the 2 points, type the distance, press record. Every point is adjusted to best fit all measurements; disagreement shows as residuals, and a reading far off the current solution is challenged before it is accepted. Edit or delete any measurement in the data sheet. The detail button overlays the interior angle at every wall corner and, for a selected point, the circles it was fixed from; while typing a new point the proposed wall's corner angle previews live.

walls, items, 3D

After the first room, walls are manual: in walls mode tap points in order (first point again closes; each room keeps its own ceiling height). In items mode create an item, then place it by two distances to a corner (or tap existing points as corners), on a wall, or drop it and drag in move mode. Measuring also works inside the 3D view - the survey pins are tappable there.

units

A number with a decimal point is metres, without is centimetres: 342 = 3.42 m, 3.42 = 3.42 m, 84 = 84 cm, 15. = 15 m.

keys

Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, double-tap empty plan to zoom in one-handed. Keyboard: digits, Enter = OK, Tab = other field, f = flip, Escape = cancel, ctrl+z undo.

bluetooth laser

Tap laser in the header to open the laser panel, then connect (Chrome, and the app must be on its https:// address or localhost). Each reading lands in the active field ready for OK. The panel also holds the remote-shoot offset and a log of recent protocol frames (copyable) so unknown meters can be given a decoder.

Meter modes: the meter's continuous mode streams into the app - the active field holds a rolling average (hand shake cancels out; re-aiming or pausing starts a fresh window), and the status line shows the sample count plus the meter's own min/max. Nothing commits until you press OK. Area and volume results are announced in the status line but never treated as distances.

Auto mode (the auto toggle in the laser row, off by default): each laser reading advances the survey by itself. The number of selected references is the contract: with 2 selected, every pair of readings places a point; with 3 or 4 selected, every point takes that many readings and commits on the last one - then the next point begins automatically against the same references. The status line always names which reference to shoot next, and the phone buzzes: short tick = reading logged, triple = point placed, long = reading refused. A mis-fired shot is retracted with del (steps back one reading); Escape restarts the current fix. Every guard still applies: impossible pairs are refused with an explanation, flip swaps a wrong side, and undo unwinds anything.

The shoot button (Bosch meters) triggers a measurement remotely - the laser arms, then fires, with no button press to shake the aim. Remote readings measure from the device's FRONT edge, so a corrective offset (the body length, default 10 cm, adjustable in the laser panel) is added to match your usual back-edge readings. The device screen stays at 0 for remote shots - compare values in the app field instead. If the meter declines, the status says so. The calibrate button in the laser panel derives the offset from the meter itself: prop it still, press its measure button once, and the app fires a remote shot and reads the body length off the difference.

Everything is saved on this device automatically. Export JSON from the log sheet for backup or to move devices.
